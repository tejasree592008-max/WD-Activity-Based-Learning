Safety Guidelines

01. Stay With Your Group

Follow your trek leader and stay on marked trails.

02. Carry Essentials

Bring sufficient water, food and a first aid kit.

03. Check The Weather

Check weather alerts before leaving for your trek.

04. Respect Nature

Do not litter, disturb wildlife or damage plants.
